## Signing

Comment the following on your first pull request:

```
I have read the CLA and I sign it.

Name:
GitHub username:
Email:
Employer (if the work was done on their time or equipment):
```

Corporate contributors — if your employer needs to sign on behalf of a team,
open an issue titled `corporate CLA` instead.

Signatures are recorded in `.github/cla-signers.json`.
